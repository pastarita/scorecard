**Hyperdimensional Vector Space Golf Scorekeeping System**

**Version**: 1.0

## Course Overview

This implementation plan structures the development of Optiforge-Dash as an 18-hole golf course, where each hole represents a feature-complete development segment. The course is divided into two nines:

- **Front Nine (Holes 1-9)**: Core MVP - Releasable product with bulk of core scope
- **Back Nine (Holes 10-18)**: Advanced features - Polish, optimization, and extended capabilities

### Shot Progression System

Each hole contains 3-5 shots that progress in semantic specificity:

1. **Drive** (Shot 1): Broad scope, low semantic clamping - Establishes foundation and architecture
2. **Approach** (Shot 2-3): Medium scope, moderate semantic clamping - Builds core functionality
3. **Chip** (Shot 4): Narrow scope, high semantic clamping - Refines and integrates
4. **Putt** (Shot 5): Minimal scope, maximum semantic clamping - Finishes with precision and quality

### Scoring System

- **Par**: Expected number of shots to complete hole
- **Birdie**: Completed in fewer shots than par
- **Eagle**: Exceptional efficiency
- **Bogey**: Required additional shots
- **Quality Check**: Each hole must pass acceptance criteria before proceeding
